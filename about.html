MEET THE FOUNDER

"Driven by a passion for Islamic art and elegant decor, Rizwan Raza Qadri brings Hanafi Collections to life with a vision of making beautiful Islamic artwork accessible to every home."

Hyderabad has long been celebrated as one of the world's eminent capitals of Islamic architecture, royal patronage, and exquisite calligraphy. Immersed in this legacy from an early age, Rizwan Raza Qadri recognized that modern luxury homes often lacked Islamic art pieces crafted to the highest international standards of museum presentation.

Hanafi Collections was born to fill that sacred space: creating frames where Arabic calligraphy is preserved in absolute orthographic accuracy, sculpted in high-relief dimensional detail, and encased in timeless baroque gilt profiles.
